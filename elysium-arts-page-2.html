What HDRP is

HDRP is your core TTRPG framework: flexible, modular, and built so the same structure can support very different worlds without losing identity.

First, the player learns the story and setting logic. Then the site explains the mechanics. After that, it introduces the three sub-modules and the larger promise: HDRP can also become the basis for an entirely original setting.

Flexible by design

The system is meant to work across different tones and campaign styles, from brutal survival to casual adventures and highly customized roleplay-heavy worlds.

The three modules

Each module gets its own small section with a title, a short descriptor, a summary paragraph, and a bullet list showing vibe and possibilities.

HOU

Heart of Umbra

A fantasy setting shaped by mystery, lore, and the tension between wonder and danger.

  • High fantasy atmosphere with strong world identity.
  • Good for epic quests, faction drama, and magical exploration.
  • Supports custom heroes, villains, and kingdoms inside the setting.
WOM

World of Madness

A sci-fi leaning world built for strange systems, pressure, and otherworldly conflict.

  • Blue-toned identity with a more surreal, human-made feel.
  • Works well for strange technology, exploration, and unstable environments.
  • Flexible enough for factions, missions, and experimental play.
ECR

Emaruvian Crusade Retold

A crusade-driven setting with apocalyptic pressure, conviction, and large-scale struggle.

  • Best for intense conflict, holy war themes, and ideological tension.
  • Supports grand campaigns and faction-heavy storytelling.
  • Good home for heroic sacrifice, duty, and end-of-era stakes.

Your own world

HDRP is not limited to the three modules. It can be converted into an original setting or adapted to a homebrew world with your own heroes, villains, factions, tone, and campaign goals.

  • Any world theme, from survival to social roleplay.
  • Any pace, from casual to grindy combat loops.
  • Any level of customization, from light tweaks to full conversion.
